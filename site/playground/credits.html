Where the files come from

This website (GitHub Pages) serves the app, the WebAssembly runtimes and the small files above. The model weights are downloaded by your browser directly from the original Hugging Face repositories, at fixed revisions, and checked against the sizes and SHA-256 hashes recorded for this release: k2-fsa/web-assembly-zh-en-tts-zipvoice @ a118980a (ZipVoice bundle), onnx-community/chatterbox-ONNX @ 3cab09af and nsarang/F5-TTS-ONNX @ 2e7e47e1. Hugging Face’s own terms and privacy policy apply to those downloads.

Our patch and build script are also under /source/, with the other changes we made (ONNX Runtime patch, decoder conversion script).

Privacy

Your recording, your text and the generated audio are processed in this browser and are not uploaded. Your browser only downloads: the app from this website, and model files from Hugging Face (the first time you use each model; nothing about your recording or text is sent with those downloads). There are no accounts, analytics or advertising.
